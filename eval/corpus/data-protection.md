# Northwind Software: Data Protection and Privacy

## Roles and agreements
Northwind acts as data processor for customer content. A GDPR-compliant Data Processing
Agreement incorporating the 2021 EU Standard Contractual Clauses is available and is signed
as part of the Enterprise order form. A UK International Data Transfer Addendum is included
for customers subject to UK GDPR.

## Hosting regions and residency
Customer data is hosted in AWS in one of three regions: eu-west-1 (Ireland), us-east-1
(N. Virginia) or ap-southeast-2 (Sydney). The region is selected at provisioning time and
data is never moved to another region without the customer's written instruction.

## Sub-processors
The current sub-processor list is published on the trust portal. Customers receive 30 days'
notice of any addition and may object in writing within that period.

## Data subject rights
Data subject access, rectification and erasure requests are supported through a
self-service API and from the administration console. The Data Protection Officer can be
reached at dpo@northwind.example.

## Retention and deletion
Customer data is deleted from production systems within 30 days of termination, and copies
held in backups are purged within 90 days. A deletion certificate is issued on request.
