# About Northwind Software

Northwind Software builds a modern workflow automation platform trusted by growing
organisations around the world. Our mission is to help teams move faster with confidence,
combining an intuitive product experience with the enterprise-grade security, reliability
and support that demanding customers expect.

## Why customers choose Northwind

Security is at the heart of everything we do. From encryption and authentication through to
auditing and compliance, the platform is designed so that administrators stay in control
and customers can meet their own regulatory obligations. Our security programme is reviewed
continuously and our certifications are maintained year after year.

Reliability matters just as much. The platform is built for availability and performance at
scale, with resilient infrastructure, disaster recovery planning and a support organisation
that treats every customer as a partner. Whatever the size of the deployment, customers can
rely on consistent service levels and transparent communication.

Integration is where the platform really shines. Open APIs, webhooks and a growing library
of native connectors mean Northwind fits into the tools teams already use, so data flows
where it is needed without manual effort or brittle custom code.

## Our commitment

We believe in long-term partnerships. From the first implementation workshop through to
ongoing customer success, our teams are measured on customer outcomes, adoption and
satisfaction. Pricing is straightforward and predictable, and licensing scales with the
organisation rather than getting in its way.
