NORTHWIND PLATFORM: ARCHITECTURE AND SERVICE LEVELS

1. DEPLOYMENT ARCHITECTURE
The Northwind platform is a multi-tenant SaaS application running on AWS. The application
tier runs as stateless containers on Amazon EKS across three availability zones, fronted by
an Application Load Balancer and AWS WAF. Tenant isolation is enforced at the data layer by
a mandatory tenant_id predicate applied in the ORM and re-checked by row-level security
policies in PostgreSQL. A single-tenant dedicated deployment is available on the Enterprise
plan for customers with regulatory isolation requirements.

2. DATA STORES
The primary datastore is Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL 16 with a Multi-AZ writer and two read
replicas. Object storage is Amazon S3 with versioning and Object Lock. Search is served by
OpenSearch. Caching and job queues use Amazon ElastiCache for Redis in cluster mode.

3. AVAILABILITY AND SLA
The contractual uptime commitment is 99.9% measured monthly for the Professional plan and
99.95% for the Enterprise plan, excluding scheduled maintenance announced 5 business days in
advance. Service credits are 10% of monthly fees for uptime below 99.9%, 25% below 99.5% and
50% below 99.0%. Measured availability over the trailing 12 months was 99.982%.

4. DISASTER RECOVERY
The Recovery Point Objective (RPO) is 5 minutes, achieved through continuous WAL archiving to
a second AWS region. The Recovery Time Objective (RTO) is 4 hours for a full regional
failover. Backups are taken continuously with point-in-time recovery for 35 days, plus
monthly snapshots retained for 12 months. Disaster recovery failover is tested twice per year
and the test report is available to Enterprise customers.

5. BUSINESS CONTINUITY
A documented Business Continuity Plan is reviewed annually and covers loss of facility, loss
of key personnel and loss of a critical supplier. Incident response follows a documented
runbook with a 15-minute acknowledgement target for Severity 1 incidents, 24x7.

6. SCALABILITY AND PERFORMANCE
The platform serves over 4,200 customer tenants and peaks at 18,000 requests per second.
Median API response time is 84 ms and the 99th percentile is 410 ms. Horizontal autoscaling
is driven by request concurrency with a 90-second scale-out window.
