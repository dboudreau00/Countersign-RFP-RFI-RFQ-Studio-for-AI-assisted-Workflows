NORTHWIND: SUPPORT AND IMPLEMENTATION

SUPPORT TIERS
Standard support is included with every plan and covers business hours in the customer's
region with a 4-hour first-response target. Premium support adds 24x7 coverage, a 1-hour
first-response target for Severity 1 incidents, and a named Technical Account Manager who
runs a monthly service review. All plans include access to the documentation portal, the
public status page and the community forum.

IMPLEMENTATION
A typical mid-market implementation takes 6 to 8 weeks: discovery (1 week), configuration
and data migration (3 to 4 weeks), integration and user acceptance testing (2 weeks), then
go-live and hypercare (1 week). Enterprise implementations with complex integrations
typically run 10 to 14 weeks. Every implementation is led by a named project manager and
includes administrator training and a train-the-trainer session for end-user champions.

CUSTOMER SUCCESS
After go-live each account is assigned a customer success manager who runs quarterly
business reviews. Adoption is tracked on a shared dashboard covering licence utilisation,
weekly active users and feature depth, and the review agenda includes a roadmap preview
and open enhancement requests.
